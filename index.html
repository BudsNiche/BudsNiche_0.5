WHY_BUDSNICHE.TXT

I wanted one place I could check every day instead of opening five different apps.

BudsNiche is a true daily check-in: know the date and weather, read a verse, get a goal, see what happened, vote on something, talk with people, then test what you know.

The goal is simple: you should feel like you haven't finished your morning until you've done your BudsNiche.

HOW_TO_PLAY.TXT

Each category gives you 45 seconds and four answers. Type them one at a time.

Obvious correct answers still count, but they score low. Specific facts and niche knowledge score higher. Wrong or unrelated answers get zero.

The point is not just knowing something about the topic. The point is showing how deep your knowledge goes.
